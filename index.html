Grid Studies

Fourteen animations on a grid of cells. Each one repeats exactly, drives its cells from a closed-form field rather than a simulation, and answers the cursor. Drag inside any panel. Click it.

Swell

A perspective ocean. Gravity waves summed at depth, faded out where the grid can no longer resolve them, with glints on the crests. Drag to leave a wake, click to drop a stone.

24s loopopen

Contour

Level sets of a drifting scalar field. Dividing the distance-to-level by the gradient keeps every line the same width, however steep the field is there. The cursor lifts the surface and the lines close around it.

24s loopopen

Cascade

A pendulum wave. Column c swings exactly 60 + c times per loop, so the array slides out of step into travelling waves, then into apparent disorder, then snaps back into a line. Press to send a flash through it.

60s loopopen

Moiré

A ripple tank. Sources drift on closed orbits and their circular fronts sum, threading the panel with the hyperbolas where they cancel. Two share a frequency and hold still; the third beats slowly against them.

24s loopopen

Cells

A Voronoi partition with sites on orbiting paths. A cell is lit by the gap between its distance to the nearest site and to the second nearest, which draws the boundaries without ever constructing them.

24s loopopen

Marble

Domain warping. The field is sampled not where the cell sits but at a point pushed around by a second field, itself pushed by a third. Two rounds of that turn plain ripples into something that folds and stretches like ink in water. Drag to stir it.

24s loopopen

Silk

A drifting height field, lit rather than measured. The surface normal comes from the analytic gradient, so the folds shade like cloth: a broad falloff with a tight sheen along each crest. The cursor lifts the fabric under it.

24s loopopen

Aurora

Four curtains, each a line whose height is a sum of travelling sines, drawn as a soft falloff either side. Where a sheet turns edge-on it narrows and brightens, and that is the fold. Drag to take hold of one.

24s loopopen

Chladni

A vibrating plate. Five standing modes are summed and what gets drawn is the set where the sum is zero: the places that never move, where sand settles on a real plate. Put a finger on it and the figure swings.

24s loopopen

Vortex

Logarithmic spiral density waves. That one combination of angle and log radius is what holds a spiral's pitch constant at every radius. Toward the centre the arms crowd past what a cell can carry and fade, which is what leaves the smooth core. Drag to shear them.

24s loopopen

Kaleido

A mirror frieze. Each cell's position is reflected about the midline, wrapped into a repeating bay, then reflected again inside it, so one field appears four ways per bay. Touch it anywhere and the touch appears in every reflection at once.

24s loopopen

Orbits

Four bodies on closed epicyclic paths, each with a tail. The tail is not remembered between frames: the path is a closed form, so a body's own past is evaluated on demand, sixteen samples back along the curve. Your cursor gets a tail of the same kind.

24s loopopen

Revival

A wave packet in a box. Energy goes as the square of the mode number, so at one particular time every phase has come round a whole number of turns at once and the packet, having spread and shattered into a lattice of copies, reassembles exactly. The loop is that revival time. Click to launch it somewhere else.

24s loopopen

Rain

Rings on still water. The shower is generated once and indexed by loop time, so it repeats exactly while being far too finely spread to catch at it. Click to add a drop.
